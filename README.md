# Fresh Beats: Music Platform Analytics Report

## Project Overview

Fresh Beats is a music streaming platform designed to help emerging artists gain visibility while offering listeners a personalized and engaging music experience. This project analyzes user behavior and genre trends to uncover insights that can increase paid subscriptions, strengthen artist promotion strategies, and enhance the platform’s overall engagement and growth.

## Introduction

In today’s fast-changing music industry, Fresh Beats connects rising artists with listeners through an innovative streaming platform. This project analyzes user engagement patterns, genre trends, and subscription behaviors to uncover opportunities that drive growth and improve retention. The ultimate goal is to strengthen artist visibility, enhance listener satisfaction, and support sustainable business performance.

## Project Objectives

1. Evaluate how effective current artist promotions are in increasing visibility and audience reach.
2. Analyze user behavior to find ways to convert free users into paid subscribers and improve engagement.
3. Identify popular genres to help emerging artists gain exposure and attract more listeners.

## Methodology

Using the provided dataset, this project analyzed user activity and genre performance to uncover patterns in engagement and subscription behavior. Insights were derived from visualizations highlighting trends in user growth and genre popularity. The process included:

**Data Preparation:** Verified and organized the dataset to ensure accuracy and consistency.
**Visualization Development:** Created clear visuals to illustrate user engagement levels and genre trends over time.
**Insight Analysis:** Interpreted key findings to support strategic decisions around user retention and artist promotion.

## Key Findings

**Genre Insights:** Rock is the most popular genre among free users, while Indie is the strongest performer among paid users. Pop peaked in 2021 but has been declining, suggesting shifting listener preferences.
**User Engagement:** Paid users listen to more tracks, show steadier growth, and have higher retention compared to free users, making them essential for sustained revenue.
**Conversion Opportunities:** Rock listeners on the free tier represent a major opportunity for conversion to paid plans through targeted promotions and trials.
**Artist Promotion:** Emerging Indie artists benefit most from visibility efforts, indicating that continued investment in promoting this genre can strengthen engagement and platform loyalty.

## Conclusion

Paid users are vital to Fresh Beats’ long-term revenue growth, making it essential to target Rock listeners in the free tier with special promotions to encourage conversion. At the same time, promoting emerging Indie artists through increased visibility and recommendation efforts will strengthen user retention and enhance overall platform engagement.
